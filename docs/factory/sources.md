# T3Factory sources and evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-29. This registry supports the [vision](vision.md). Inclusion means inspiration to evaluate, not adoption, permission to copy source, runtime validation, or verified parity. The [full inspiration register](https://github.com/baladithyab/t3code-factory/issues/1) retains prior investigations; active decisions belong in their owning issues.

## Foundations and inspected implementation

The owner's direction is one thread-based experience for software, research, and knowledge work, with useful parallel delegation and collaboration. Project coordination is an optional role; remote sandbox vendors share a capability-based category.

| [Sentry Junior](https://github.com/getsentry/junior/tree/50f3168814824bf4aa0834f2894fc0153069bbf6), [docs](https://junior.sentry.dev/), [recorded research](https://github.com/baladithyab/t3code-factory/issues/1#issuecomment-6512606153) | Adapt thread-bound watches, durable automations, pre-agent filters/run caps, and resolution Briefs. Junior is distinct from Seer/Autofix. Credential injection and Guardian review merit study; an LLM reviewer does not replace deterministic authority checks. Source/docs review only. |

Research used three independent lanes through Claude, OMP, and Codex, followed by a separate architecture critique and source review of project coordination. The synthesis changed several suggestions: one shared UX, dependency-based readiness instead of phase barriers, addressed durable messages, preserved claims during cancellation, and one common sandbox category. These were design/source investigations, not integration experiments or product acceptance tests.
